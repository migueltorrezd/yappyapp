# YappYapp

register: product

## Product Purpose

Local-first macOS menu-bar dictation. Records the mic, transcribes on-device
with Parakeet or whisper.cpp, applies a personal glossary, and types the result
wherever the cursor is. No cloud transcription, no API keys, and no transcript
history on disk. Dictation audio and transcript content stay on this Mac;
model downloads and update checks are the documented network exceptions.

## Users

- Heavy daily dictators: people who dictate posts, client messages, and AI
  coding-assistant prompts all day, in English, French, Spanish, and Dutch.
  They often whisper to avoid disturbing the room.
- Teams on managed Macs: non-technical operators who get the app as a DMG.
  They need zero-terminal setup and obvious affordances.

## Brand & Tone

Confident, direct, slightly irreverent. The app says "Listening" and "Done in
0.8s", never "Please wait while we process your request". Privacy claims are
stated as facts, not marketing.

YappYapp has one visual register:
- "Brutal": loud, flat, thick-bordered, saturated, and built from hard offset
  shadows. Very opinionated. Personality over politeness. No translucency,
  blur, gradients, or soft shadows anywhere.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (soft shadows, gray-on-white, rounded everything).
- Apple Settings clone minimalism: the product surface must NOT look native.
- Crypto-neon-on-black and "AI gradient purple" cliches.

## Strategic Principles

- One-glance state: recording vs ready vs needs-setup must read instantly.
- Everything reachable in two clicks from the menu bar icon.
- The waveform is the soul of the app; it must feel alive in the Brutal surface.
- Compliance posture is a feature: in-memory history, local transcription, and
  network access restricted to documented model downloads and update checks.
